PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) Scheme Guidelines (sample source text for seeding)

Objective: To provide income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country.

Benefits: Under the scheme an amount of Rs.6000/- per year is released in three equal
installments of Rs.2000/- each, every four months.

Eligibility: All landholding farmers' families, subject to certain exclusions, are eligible
to receive benefit under the scheme. A "family" is defined as husband, wife and minor
children who have cultivable landholding as per land records of the concerned State/UT.

Exclusions: The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status shall not
be eligible for benefit under the scheme: all Institutional Landholders; farmer families
in which one or more of its members belong to the following categories: former and present
holders of constitutional posts; former and present Ministers/State Ministers; persons who
paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.

Required documents: Aadhaar card (for linkage only, not stored by this application), land
record documents, and bank account details for direct benefit transfer.
