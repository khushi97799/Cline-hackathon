Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) Guidelines (sample source text for seeding)

Objective: To provide LPG (cooking gas) connections to women from Below Poverty Line (BPL)
households.

Benefits: A free LPG connection with financial assistance for the first refill and stove,
provided to eligible women applicants.

Eligibility: The applicant must be a woman above 18 years of age. The applicant's household
should belong to a BPL family as per the State/UT list, or belong to one of the specified
SECC-2011 deprivation categories. The applicant should not already have an LPG connection
in her name in any oil marketing company.

Required documents: proof of age, proof of address, BPL certificate or SECC list inclusion
proof, and bank account details for subsidy transfer.
