National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) - Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme
(IGNOAPS) Guidelines (sample source text for seeding)

Objective: To provide old age pension to elderly persons belonging to Below Poverty Line
(BPL) households.

Benefits: A monthly pension amount as notified by the State/UT government, co-funded by
the Central Government, paid directly to the beneficiary's bank or post office account.

Eligibility: The applicant must be 60 years of age or above. The applicant's household must
belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household as per the criteria determined by the
respective State/UT government.

Required documents: age proof, BPL certificate, bank or post office account passbook copy,
and a recent passport-size photograph.
