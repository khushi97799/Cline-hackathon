About Scheme Sniper

Many people miss government schemes because the rules are hard to read and the offices are far away. Scheme Sniper helps a person see which schemes may fit them, which documents they already have, and exactly where to go next.

Our promises

✔ Results come from fixed rules written from official text, never from guesses.

✔ We never say "you qualify". We say what is likely and what to confirm at the office.

✔ Every scheme shows its source and the date it was last verified.

✔ We do not store document images, audio or Aadhaar numbers.

✔ Offices, addresses and steps come only from the data files we hold. We do not make them up.

What this is not

This is not a government website and it does not submit applications to government portals. The submit and receipt screens are a labelled simulation.
